6 Days / Week

Push • Pull • Legs (PPL)

Best for intermediate to advanced lifters targeting maximum hypertrophy.

  • Push: Chest, Front Delts, Triceps
  • Pull: Lats, Upper Back, Rear Delts, Biceps
  • Legs: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves

Arnold Golden Era Split

High intensity antagonist superset style for aesthetic shape and pump.

  • Day 1 & 4: Chest & Back
  • Day 2 & 5: Shoulders & Arms (Biceps/Triceps)
  • Day 3 & 6: Legs & Lower Back
