Training Blueprints

Proven Workout Splits

Target each muscle group with optimal frequency and recovery.

6 Days / Week

Push • Pull • Legs (PPL)

Best for building pure muscle mass and balance across the physique.

  • Push: Chest, Front Delts, Triceps
  • Pull: Lats, Upper Back, Rear Delts, Biceps
  • Legs: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves
The Golden Era

Arnold Antagonist Split

High volume superset routine for upper-body V-taper and arm thickness.

  • Day 1 & 4: Chest & Back
  • Day 2 & 5: Shoulders & Arms (Bi/Tri)
  • Day 3 & 6: Legs & Core
4 Days / Week

Upper • Lower Split

Ideal for busy professionals who want maximum strength with proper rest.

  • Monday: Upper Body (Power)
  • Tuesday: Lower Body (Power)
  • Thursday: Upper Body (Hypertrophy)
  • Friday: Lower Body (Hypertrophy)
